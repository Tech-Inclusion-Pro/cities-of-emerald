## The family (proposal — the last open decision)

The bell house is House 3, mid-town, the small bell by its door.

- **Tomas** — Kai's childhood best friend. Survived the storm,
  built a life on the water, rang the bell every year on the day
  of the storm ("so anyone still out there could find their way
  home"). Passed away years ago; he is present only in stories.
- **Marisol** (she/her) — Tomas's daughter. Grew up on the stories;
  keeps the bell rung. She is the one who recognized the Archivist
  years ago and was afraid of what he had become; the heroic act is
  what lets her open the door.
- **Nico and Bay** — Marisol's children, the ones who grew up
  hearing about brave, stubborn, kind Kai from before the sea.
  They are the ones who say the name, because to them it was never
  lost — it was a bedtime story.

## The scene

After V5 (rift shut, storm stopped, quiet boat ride home), the
family stands outside the bell house; the Archivist is at the dock,
not leaving, not approaching. Talking to him starts it: the
children run to him; Nico says the name — "You're KAI! Grandpa
TOMAS told us everything!" — and Bay rings the bell, once. Marisol
says the hard parts plainly: they searched, they found him, they
were afraid; Tomas never stopped telling the stories. Kai answers
with the acceptance the whole thread has been building: he cannot
have the past back, and the people and memories he loved continued
— here, on the water. Sets **FLAG_CITIES_KAI_NAMED**.

## After the name (the epilogue states)

- **Kai** returns to the Weather Institute. His desk faces the sea
  now — the payoff of Halley's planted line. He stands at the 2F
  window; his dialogue is quiet, forward-looking, and signed with
  his name. Halley gets one last line.
- **The annex** becomes the Coralmere Archive — a memorial and a
  school. **Meridian keeps it** (she teaches now; her Gardevoir
  minds the children). **Gnomon dismantles the machines himself**;
  he is there too, cataloguing parts into crates labeled "NEVER
  AGAIN" and, underneath, "PROBABLY."
- **The family** afterward: the kids retell the scene; Marisol's
  door is open.
