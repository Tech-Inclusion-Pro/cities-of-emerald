## The shape of the thread

Five arcs, played in order, levels 60/65/70/75/80 (GDD 6.4). Each
arc = story scenes + its legendaries as encounters (M1-pattern
statics or scripted battles). Team Epoch is values-driven: every
scene shows people who believe they are saving someone. The player
is never fighting evil — they are slowly meeting a grief.

## Cast [PROPOSED]

- **The Archivist** — leader. Appearance: a quiet, weathered man in
  a long slate-gray coat, always carrying a small brass barometer
  (Coralmere's old harbor instrument — its meaning lands in V4).
  Implementation: vanilla stand-in sprites for now (overworld
  GENTLEMAN-class silhouette, existing trainer-class front pic);
  custom art is a release-phase [PROPOSED], not a blocker.
- **Admin Meridian** (she/her) — the believer. Joined because the
  Archivist once saved her town's records from a flood; she follows
  the man, not the plan. In the ending she is the first to stand
  down.
- **Admin Gnomon** (he/him) — the engineer. Built the rift
  machinery; treats the past as a technical problem. His V5 doubt
  ("we can hold the door open — I never asked if we should walk
  through") foreshadows the ending.
- **Epoch Grunts** — archivist-styled: coats, satchels, gloves.
  They catalogue, they don't menace. Battle lines are about
  preserving and remembering, never conquering.

## V1 — "Products and Targets" (Mewtwo, Mew, Genesect) — level 60

- Epoch quietly buys research: a lab site near the Mossdeep Space
  Center annex [PROPOSED location]. They seek Pokémon strong enough
  to hold a tear in time open: Mewtwo (made), Genesect (rebuilt),
  Mew (the template).
- Beats: strange purchases reported → the player finds the annex →
  first meeting with Meridian (courteous, unreadable) → Mewtwo
  freed from containment (encounter), Genesect deactivated then
  wakes (encounter), Mew appears only after both are caught —
  drawn to the player, not the lab.
- Tone: the lab is clean and careful. Epoch hurt no one. That is
  what makes it unsettling.

## V2 — "Stolen Research" (Type: Null, Silvally, Magearna) — level 65

- Epoch takes what it cannot buy: Devon's vault and a private
  collection lose three "synthetic hearts."
- Beats: Devon asks the Champion for help → trail to an Epoch
  field camp (Route 115 cliffs [PROPOSED]) → Gnomon debuts,
  apologetic and precise → Type: Null encounter; Silvally after
  it; Magearna wakes when both are caught and CHOOSES the player
  (it walked out of Epoch's hands — the first crack in the plan).
- The Archivist is heard, not seen: a letter left for the player,
  the first plain-language statement of WHY (a village, a storm,
  a warning that came too late).

## V3 — "The Wrong Doors" (Ultra Beasts, Cosmog line, Solgaleo, Lunala, Necrozma, Poipole, Naganadel) — level 70

- The first rift attempts fail: instead of the past, the machines
  open Ultra Wormholes. Hoenn gets visitors.
- Beats: wormholes over several sites (existing maps; one per UB
  group) → Epoch triages ITS OWN disaster alongside the player —
  grunts helping civilians, Meridian coordinating → the Archivist
  appears in person at last: he closes a wormhole by hand,
  apologizes to the player, and does not stop. Cosmog is found and
  shelters with the player; its line, the light legends, and the
  UBs are encountered across the cleanup. Reward: N-Solarizer +
  N-Lunarizer (GDD).
- This is the arc where the player sees Epoch is not the villain —
  the plan is.

## V4 — "The Day of the Storm" (Paradox Pokémon, Koraidon, Miraidon, Terapagos) — level 75

- Epoch breaks through — but sideways: time RIFTS, past and future
  spilling into the present. Paradox Pokémon roam the rift zones.
- The heart of the game: the Coralmere time-rift scene. The player
  walks the village before the storm (12.4's past map): the docks,
  the bell tower, the small streets. The Archivist stands in the
  middle of it, not moving, holding the barometer. The scene ends
  when the rift closes on its own — the past cannot be held.
- After the scene, the journal gains the Coralmere survivors entry
  ([PROPOSED] in GDD, recommend confirming); the survivor hints
  across Hoenn now read differently.
- Encounters: paradox species in rift pockets; Koraidon/Miraidon
  as the rift's anchors; Terapagos at the deepest point.

## V5 — "The Anchor" (Eternatus) — level 80

- Epoch taps Eternatus to make the change permanent — and loses
  control. The storm that gathers heads for Pacifidlog, echoing
  the storm that took Coralmere.
- Beats: evacuation scene at Pacifidlog (the family with the bell
  by their door) → the player and the Archivist fight THROUGH the
  storm together (Eternatus encounter at the rift core) → **the
  heroic act** (GDD [PROPOSED], recommend confirming): the
  Archivist shuts the rift down to save Pacifidlog, giving up
  Coralmere, not knowing his friend's family lives there.
- **The ending (all [DECIDED]):** the family approaches. The
  children speak his name — Kai. From that scene on, the name flag
  flips and he is Kai everywhere. Team Epoch changes direction:
  the archive becomes a memorial and a school. Meridian keeps it;
  Gnomon dismantles the machines himself.
