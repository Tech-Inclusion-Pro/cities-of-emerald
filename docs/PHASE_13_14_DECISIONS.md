# Phase 13–14 Open Decisions (for approval)

**Status: APPROVED 2026-09-17 (all four as proposed).**

The remaining [OPEN]/[PROPOSED] design items between here and the
challenge modes. Settling these now means Phases 12→14 build without
a stop.

## 1. Mythical event levels (GDD 6.6, [OPEN] — "ask before setting")

All 14 mythical events unlock after the League. **[PROPOSED]: one
flat rule — every mythical encounter is fixed level 70.** Between
the hidden-area tiers, high enough to feel special, and one rule to
remember instead of fourteen. (Alternative: tier them 60–80 by
"power fantasy" — more flavor, more to playtest.)

## 2. Shiny Charm (GDD 14.4 [PROPOSED])

**[PROPOSED]: Birch gives the Shiny Charm at 300 caught species.**
With ~950 species in the dex, the vanilla "complete the National
Dex" rule would push the charm out of reach of nearly everyone —
300 keeps it a real milestone that a dedicated player actually
reaches (accessibility-first). (Alternative: full dex, purist.)

## 3. Move relearner / deleter (GDD 14.5 [PROPOSED])

**[PROPOSED]: both NPCs side by side in Mauville's central plaza
(the game's hub), both free — no Heart Scales.** The vanilla pair
(Fallarbor relearner, Lilycove deleter) stay where they are too;
the Mauville pair is the QoL copy.

## 4. Champion defense (GDD 14.1 [OPEN])

**[PROPOSED]: title defenses reuse the national rankings cast.**
After becoming Champion, an official in the Ever Grande lobby
offers a "title defense" any time: a full battle against a
challenger drawn from the ranked-NPC roster (Vesper, Corsen,
Ilaria, Bram, and the leaderboard cast), with postgame-tier teams.
Wins score ranking points; the Master event already sits on top of
this system, so the feature is mostly assembly, not new machinery.
